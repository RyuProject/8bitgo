QEMU is GPL-2.0 (some libraries LGPL-2.1); license copies are in licenses/.
The Alpine demo repository is Apache-2.0; its guest image also includes Alpine packages with their respective licenses.
xterm.js 5.3.0 and xterm-pty 0.12.0 are MIT; license copies are in licenses/.
This site uses the upstream QEMU build and guest image without modifying those binary files.
The site-specific launcher is start.js, which boots the original Alpine image without the upstream demo network proxy.
