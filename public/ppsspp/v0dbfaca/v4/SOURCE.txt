8BitGo applies vendor/ppsspp/patches/0001 through 0008 before building. They add strict
HTTP Range streaming, pthread-safe WebGL and audio, the save/control bridge, and the
adaptive browser performance profile, plus measured CHD cold-start and preload-data
optimizations. The pinned source plus those patches reproduce the distributed WASM files;
scripts/build-ppsspp.mjs is the canonical build entry point.
